Ảnh được tạo bằng công cụ imagegen tích hợp. Prompt gốc:

Design one original cute baby red panda mascot named Mơ. Friendly, spirited study buddy, full body sitting and lifting one paw in a small wave. Warm but boldly graphic. Chunky rounded contours and a confident warm burgundy outline, flat cut-paper layers, only very slight paper texture inset inside the colored shapes, and compact ivory sticker edge. Should read clearly at 48-pixel scale, with bold clear silhouette and simple uncluttered facial expression. Centered, single figure filling about 85% of a square canvas with margins around all limbs and tail. Genuine transparent background outside silhouette, no backdrop, no enclosing badge, no ground shadow, no letters, no marks, no other characters. The character is a red panda with its own visual identity.
