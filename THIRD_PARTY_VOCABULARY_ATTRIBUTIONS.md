# Third-party vocabulary attribution

HustLingo production vocabulary architecture is designed to interoperate with corpus-based open wordlists from the NGSL Project.

## NGSL Project

New General Service List (NGSL), New Academic Word List (NAWL), TOEIC Service List (TSL), and Business Service List (BSL) are published by the NGSL Project and are available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Project: https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

This patch does not claim that TOEIC or IELTS publishes an official required vocabulary list. Exam fields in HustLingo are relevance metadata for learning and practice.

## CMU Pronouncing Dictionary

Where available in the build environment, pronunciation metadata is normalized from CMUdict-derived ARPAbet and converted to IPA-like notation for offline learner display. Review pronunciation before publication where accent-specific precision is required.
